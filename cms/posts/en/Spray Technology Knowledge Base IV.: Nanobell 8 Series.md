**Sames**' new generation of rotary bell atomizers sets a new benchmark for demanding industrial applications, offering exceptional performance, precision, and efficiency. Available from September 2025.

For decades, the automotive industry has represented the gold standard in robotic paint application, requiring uncompromising quality and maximum efficiency. As a driving force in technological innovation, Sames has played a key role in shaping this field.

With the expansion of the Nanocoat product line—including the Nanobell 801, 803, and 805—Sames now brings this expertise to a wider range of industries, opening the door to unmatched levels of performance and profitability.

#### **Maximum Performance Through Precise Spray Pattern Control**

Independent air-flow adjustment ensures optimal atomization and exceptional control over the spray pattern, allowing perfect adaptation to any industrial requirement.

The new T8 air motor delivers stable and precise rotational speed, regardless of flow rate, guaranteeing consistent, high-quality production.

Rotary bell technology can replace up to three spray guns, thanks to its atomization capacity of up to 800 cm³/min.

Adjustable spray pattern size—enabled by the NW air deflectors—allows accurate adaptation to complex geometries, ensuring perfect part coverage while minimizing overspray.(NW = Narrow/Wide, Sames’ newly patented atomization air management system.)

#### **Nanobell 801 – Non-electrostatic applications**

* High bell speed for fine atomization
* Excellent transfer efficiency enabled by the patented NW air-control system
* Simple installation and easy maintenance

Combination of straight and Vortex airflows for optimal material delivery and superior finish quality

#### **Proven heritage, next-generation technology**

The Nanobell 8 Series builds on the proven reliability and productivity of earlier Sames models (such as the PPH308 and PPH707), while introducing the latest engineering advancements.

With these innovations, Sames now enables not only automotive but a wide range of other industries to benefit from cutting-edge rotary bell coating technology.
