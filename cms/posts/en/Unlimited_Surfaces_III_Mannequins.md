Mannequins — those silent, sometimes eerie sentinels of shop windows — are far more than simple clothing stands. Each one is the result of a meticulous manufacturing process, where painting plays a key role. The flawless surface, the shades, and the textures all ensure that the garments they display look their absolute best.

The world of mannequins is incredibly diverse:

* They can be made of fiberglass-reinforced polyester, offering durability and a perfectly smooth, paint-ready surface.
* Others are fabric-covered tailoring mannequins, where the color and texture of the material are crucial.
* And there are bendable, metal-framed models designed to hold creative poses for visual displays.

Their shapes and forms are nearly limitless — male, female, child, athletic, classic, maternity, or even torso-only models focusing on details. What all of them share is that the quality of the paintwork defines the final impression. The surface must be immaculate, and the colors vivid, as these elements form the foundation of an eye-catching display.

Our connection to this fascinating industry began through a collaboration with Figura Dekor, a renowned Budapest-based company. As part of the project, we installed a Blowtherm IDSW dry-separation spray booth, providing clean, safe, and efficient painting conditions throughout the production process.

The result: smooth, durable, and visually striking surfaces — a worthy foundation for the “fashion shows” that take place in shop windows every day.
