The story of Dewa Zrt. began in 1990, when three former colleagues – Péter Buczkó, József Ursz, and later buyout partner Géza Bradács – decided to leave the state sector and start their own company, involving Péter’s wife, Ariela De Ambrosi. Drawing on their expertise and market experience from their previous workplace, HAFE (Gear and Painting Equipment Factory), they launched a private painting business under the name Dewa Kft.

Operations initially started in Békéscsaba, but rapid growth soon required a second headquarters. While József handled eastern region projects from Békéscsaba, Péter established the company’s western hub in Budaörs. Both locations operate in similar buildings on separate sites, reflecting the company’s unity in both appearance and operations.

The Békéscsaba team started as a one-person operation, with József managing daily tasks alone for years. As the company grew, a stable and reliable team became necessary, providing crucial support. Today, the team has grown to over ten members, including service technicians, engineers, and family members – among them József’s two children and one daughter-in-law. Over time, the headquarters has become increasingly family-oriented, where intergenerational collaboration is not only a core value but also a key factor in long-term development.

Next, we will take a closer look at the Békéscsaba headquarters, exploring how younger József Ursz joined daily management and how he is preparing to carry forward his father’s professional and personal legacy.

The Expansion of the Békéscsaba Team and the Involvement of József Ursz Jr.

József Ursz Jr. joined the daily operations of Dewa Zrt. 16 years ago, when his father invited him to work at the company: “Try it, see how you feel, but it’s not mandatory,” he was told. Until then, he had been a professional athlete, playing competitive football, so his interest in the company’s activities was limited. However, the business was not unfamiliar to him, as he had spent summers with the founding families since childhood and had seen firsthand its success. He recalls:

“Sixteen years ago, I started as a helper assembling our equipment, which allowed me to learn the setup process firsthand. I learned service techniques and material preparation from Oszkár Kellner, our chief service technician at the time. Later, András Kovács, our logistics specialist, introduced me to logistics and financial tasks. I owe a lot to both professionally and personally.”

Initially, navigating the company’s diverse activities was challenging, but he quickly grasped the interconnections and operational logic. Over the years, he gained increasing insight and expertise. Today, he leads the warehouse and service teams, while also overseeing logistics and financial responsibilities.

Growth After Joining – The Emergence of a New Leader

Joining a family business often comes with challenges, but in the Ursz family’s case, it happened naturally and smoothly. His father recalls:

“It probably went so well because Józsi and I have always had a great relationship – finding common ground was never a problem. The service team welcomed him immediately, which helped a lot. I could see he was genuinely interested and serious about his work, so I gradually entrusted him with more responsibility. Today, he has worked his way up the ladder, and I trust him completely – both professionally and personally. If clients contact me because of my connections, I redirect them to József without hesitation, confident he can handle the business.”

Reaching this point required significant professional growth and a shift in mindset. What was once just a job for József Jr. has now become a vision for the future: he recognizes that Dewa Zrt. offers not only opportunity but also long-term prospects – for himself, his children, and potentially even future generations.

Family Roles and Values at the Headquarters – Looking Toward the Future

As mentioned earlier, several members of the Ursz family are actively involved at the Békéscsaba headquarters: alongside younger József, his sister Judit and his wife Lejla contribute to the company’s operations. Judit handles legal matters as the company’s lawyer, while Lejla focuses on business development, leveraging her network and excellent communication skills.

Although the family works together daily, they consciously separate work from private life: weekend lunches are for personal connection, not business. Yet the family atmosphere permeates the headquarters, creating a friendly and cheerful environment where colleagues treat each other as friends and form a true community. József reflects:

“I believe this is the company’s greatest value – the trust and cohesion we share. That’s why I give everyone freedom in their roles, knowing I can rely on them. I’m convinced that Dewa is a golden opportunity: a business that will always have demand, regardless of market or technological changes. With good leadership and continuous development, Dewa can thrive for generations – and it’s a joy to see József gradually ready to take over. I trust wholeheartedly that under his guidance, the company and the Békéscsaba headquarters will not only preserve their values but also move toward an even brighter future.”
