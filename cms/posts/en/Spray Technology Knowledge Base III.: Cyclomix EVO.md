Cyclomix Evo is one of the latest systems from Sames — more compact, more modern and smarter than its predecessors. Designed with efficiency, safety and user experience at its core,

**it delivers the following key advantages for its users:**

#### **Easy Access, Fast Maintenance**

During development, a key design priority was to ensure that all components are easily accessible and quickly replaceable. Every connection is fully exposed, enabling fast, convenient maintenance and part replacement. This significantly reduces downtime and improves overall production efficiency.

#### **Modular Architecture and Flexible Connections**

The material-handling section is physically separated from the control unit and connected via compressed-air hoses. The hose length can be adjusted to suit the local installation layout, providing maximum flexibility. The internal design of the control module follows the same modular philosophy: it contains only the elements actually required for operation, simplifying both use and maintenance.

![](/szorasi_tipusok/cyclomix2.png)

#### **Compatibility and Advanced Data Management**

The new controller is fully compatible with previous Cyclomix generations, making modernization straightforward and cost-effective. With advanced data acquisition and processing capabilities, monitoring production parameters and verifying process efficiency becomes far easier — offering long-term operational advantages.

#### **Tailored Configurations Based on Customer Needs**

The system can be configured according to customer requirements, supporting everything from conventional Airspray to Airmix™ and Airless technologies. It also enables the simultaneous operation of two spray guns and can manage up to 99 base colors and B components, offering unmatched flexibility for any production environment.

#### **Innovative Technologies for Maximum Efficiency**

One of the standout features of the new system is the patented “atomized component injection” technology, which introduces the B component directly into the paint stream, ensuring perfect mixing.

Material savings during color changes are achieved through the Paint Optimization Mode, which minimizes waste while increasing coating quality.

#### **User-Friendly Interface and Remote Control**

The touchscreen interface displays only the information relevant to the operator at any given moment, ensuring simple and intuitive use. Access levels can be modified at any time, and clear pictograms guide the user through each function. The system can store virtually unlimited formulas, and key functions can be controlled remotely via a smartphone application. This increases convenience, flexibility, managerial oversight, and overall operational safety and cost-efficiency.

**The Technology of Tomorrow — Available Today**

The new Sames system is more than a piece of equipment; it is a complete, next-generation solution designed to meet all expectations of modern manufacturing. Faster, smarter, and fully customizable — this is not just a machine, but the next evolution in coating technology.
