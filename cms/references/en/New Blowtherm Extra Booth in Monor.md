The year 2023 began with the successful completion of another project. At the newly established Highway Engineering Depot, Dewa Zrt. delivered a high-capacity Blowtherm Extra spray booth designed to meet every requirement. The unit features increased interior height, width, and length, ready to bring more color to everyday operations. Its heavy-duty floor structure allows for the painting of vehicles weighing up to 16 tons. Equipped with our latest control system, the booth simplifies both operator tasks and maintenance scheduling. Despite its robust construction, the structure requires no additional support frame, thanks to its self-supporting panel design.
