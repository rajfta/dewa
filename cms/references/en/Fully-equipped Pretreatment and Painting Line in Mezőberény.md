Our company recently delivered one of its latest fully engineered coating lines in Mezőberény. The workpieces are pretreated through an automatic spray tunnel, then transferred to the appropriate spray booth according to the technological requirements. After passing through the combined painting and drying booths, the coated parts move into a cooling zone, where the finished products are removed. The pretreatment and painting processes are further supported by mobile dust extraction units and a dedicated paint-mixing room, ensuring clean, efficient, and high-quality operation.
