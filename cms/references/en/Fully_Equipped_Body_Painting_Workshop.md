In 2021, our company built a fully equipped body painting workshop for the newly constructed Škoda and Volkswagen dealership and service center in Budaörs. The facility includes multiple spray booths of various sizes, preparation stations with integrated lifts, and a central paint-mixing room to support efficient workflow. The entire workspace was designed to ensure optimized and seamless painting processes.

## A Fully Equipped Workshop with Premium Technology

Our client’s requirement was clear: a comprehensive workspace capable of handling every stage of the painting process. Accordingly, the following equipment was installed:

* 2 × **Blowtherm Extra** spray booths
* 1 × **Blowtherm Genius** two-station preparation area
* 1 × **Blowtherm Genius+** four-station preparation zone with motorized curtain and lift
* 1 × **Blowtherm Mixing Box** paint-mixing room

During the design process, we strictly focused on three core principles:

environmental awareness, energy efficiency, and technological workflow.

## Environmental Awareness and Energy Efficiency

The first two principles are ensured by the premium Italian brand Blowtherm, whose products we proudly distribute:

* **Activated carbon post-filtration unit:** Air leaving the spray booth — filled with overspray — first passes through fiberglass Paint Stop filters, while evaporated solvents are captured by a specialized post-filtration system. This prevents environmentally harmful emissions from entering the atmosphere.
* **Process-controlled direct-fired gas burner:** The Blowtherm GV/VA gas burner series is exceptional in its process control, consuming up to 25% less gas than conventional burners. Its nearly perfect combustion eliminates the need for a chimney or additional emission permits.

> Both of these technologies can be integrated into any of our spray booths or preparation stations upon request. We highly recommend them to partners engaged in continuous production — and to anyone prioritizing low operating costs and environmental sustainability.

## Optimized Technological Workflow

For the third principle, technological workflow, we worked closely with our client to precisely design the paint shop’s operations. We took into account the varying sizes of vehicles and the expected process durations.

The result is a workspace designed for smooth, uninterrupted progression, allowing all stages — from preparation and painting to polishing — to operate efficiently without interfering with one another.
