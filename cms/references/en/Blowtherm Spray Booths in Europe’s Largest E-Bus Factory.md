In 2017, Europe’s largest electric bus manufacturing plant was established in Komárom, where we installed multiple **Blowtherm** spray booths in several phases. The project included the installation of a climate-controlled painting and drying booth equipped with a 3D painting platform, a small-parts spray booth, a chassis painting booth, and in 2021, the addition of two preparation stations. Each unit was designed to meet the specific requirements of the different production stages, ensuring efficiency, precision, and outstanding surface quality.
