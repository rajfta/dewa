This project involved the installation of an integrated paint booth and vehicle preparation system designed to accommodate larger vehicles while improving workflow efficiency and reducing operating costs. Particular emphasis was placed on energy efficiency, operational flexibility and the creation of a safe, compliant working environment.

## Key features of the Installed Blowtherm Paint Booth and Preparation Area

🔸 PLC-controlled paint booth

🔸 Hybrid electric and gas-fired air handling system

🔸 Three-bay preparation area with curtain-separated workstations and a 5 × 2 m paint mixing room

🔸 Heat recovery system, LED lighting and independent ventilation system

🔸 Multiple vehicle lifts with a lifting capacity of 3.5 tonnes

## Results Achieved:

✅ Enhanced operational efficiency and lower operating costs

✅ Reduced energy consumption and environmental footprint

✅ Reliable operation and improved productivity

✅ Compliance with industry-specific technical and occupational safety requirements

✅ Greater flexibility to accommodate varying repair processes and workload demands
